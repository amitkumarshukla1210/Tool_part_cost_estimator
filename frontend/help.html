How the Part Cost Estimator works

Upload a STEP file, check the inputs, and read the results stage by stage. Every rate and rule comes from Master data.

0

Projects & parts

On Dashboard create a project and a variant, then Import a STEP part or assembly (split automatically) or many parts with the Excel template. Parts keeps every revision and its history.

Yellow fields are yours to change. Defaults come from master data, and results update as you type.

Grey italic cells are calculated. Bold rows with a dark line above are the totals that matter.

Stage A

Geometry & net weight

ResultHow it is calculated
VolumeExact solid volume from the CAD kernel (OpenCascade)
Net part weightVolume (cm³) × density (g/cm³)
Bounding box L × W × HTight box along the model's X/Y/Z axes, L ≥ W ≥ H
Projected areaShadow of the part along X, Y and Z without through-holes; the largest is the opening direction

Export the part in its moulding / casting orientation. CAD units must be millimetres.

Stage C

Cavities & number of tools

StepFormula
Planned hours / yearWorking days × shifts × hours per shift
Productive hours / yearPlanned hours × OEE (OEE is applied once, here)
Cavities neededAnnual volume ÷ productive hours × cycle time (s) ÷ 3600
Cavities per toolNeeded, rounded up to an allowed count; no more than fit on the plate or the maximum
Tools in parallelceil(cavities needed ÷ cavities per tool)
Tools over programmemax(tools in parallel, ceil(shots over programme ÷ tool life))
Machine utilisation(shots × cycle ÷ 3600 ÷ OEE) ÷ (planned hours × tools in parallel)

Plastic cycle time is estimated from the main wall: base time + cooling factor × wall². Replace it with your own figure.

ValueFormula (stored in Master data → Countries, machines & formulas)

Depreciation(Price − residual) ÷ life years ÷ machine hours
Interest(Price + residual) ÷ 2 × interest % ÷ machine hours
PowerkW × load factor × electricity rate
Maintenance · floor space · consumablesPrice × maintenance % ÷ hours · m² × rent × 12 ÷ hours · rate per hour
MHRMachine cost subtotal × (1 + overhead %)
Labour rateSalary × 12 × (1 + employer %) ÷ (days × hours per shift) × (1 + supervision %) ÷ productive %

Every rate has monthly versions. Only approved versions are used, and approved versions are never changed: a new value is a new version (Draft, or Save & approve). Click any number on the Rates page to see its formula, inputs, sources, approval and history.

Master data

Keeping rates and rules up to date

General master data · Excel

master_data/master_data.xlsx has three sheets: Parameters (defaults and rules), Materials (density, RM rate, scrap value) and Machines (tonnage, tie-bar size, MHR).

Mould & die database · SQLite

master_data/costing_db.sqlite has mould bases, tool steels, BOM elements with rules, process rates and sizing rules. Export it to Excel, edit it, and import it back.

Saving is checked

A wrong value is refused with its sheet and row number, the row is highlighted, and the file stays unchanged.

Writing rules

One-line formulas such as max(4, ceil(cavities * projected_area_cm2 / 8)) or cavities if hot_runner else 0. The list of variables is on the Master data page.
