Family tools

One mould or die for 2–4 parts. Same shot (injection, EPS, die casting, blow, foundry moulds and sheet-metal dies): every shot / stroke makes all parts, one base takes the cavities of every part. Swap inserts (sheet metal, stage or progressive): one common die set, the parts run one after another and only the inserts / changed stations are swapped. Each part keeps its own RM, drawings and outputs; its share of the family tool goes into its part cost and CAPEX (switch per part: family tool / own tool).
