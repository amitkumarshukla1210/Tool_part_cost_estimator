Master data · price master

Price master: month-wise prices by country

Tool materials and part raw materials (type and grade), and the scrap value, month by month for each country. Type a price under a month to save it as a new version from that month on; old versions are kept, so stored costs only change when you recalculate them. Enter next month's prices in the last column. Tool and part costing use the latest approved price on or before their costing month. A month without a price shows the earlier one (grey). New grades: Tool BOM → + New grade, or Master data → Tool materials / Materials. Standard parts keep their catalogue price (Master data → Mould & die database → Standard parts).

Prices you save and approve are approved in your name.
